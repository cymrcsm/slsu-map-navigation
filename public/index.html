Kiosk Quick Guide

    How to Navigate the Campus

    Follow these quick steps to find your destination:

    1
    Search or Browse

    Start typing an office, room code, building or acronym — matching places appear as you type. Or pick a category on the right to list everything in it.

    2
    Tap Location Pins

    Touch any location pin icon on the map to view room details, office hours, and descriptions.

    3
    Ask for Directions

    Select any destination pin and tap Ask for Directions to view an active route line from your current kiosk position.

    4
    Reset View

    Tap the auto-center button at the bottom of the control strip, to the right of the map, anytime to return to the full campus overview.
